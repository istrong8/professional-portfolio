# Engineering Design, Innovation & Interdisciplinary Projects

This section documents independently developed engineering concepts, design studies, computational prototypes, systems analyses, and interdisciplinary technical investigations.

The purpose is to demonstrate creative engineering problem-solving supported by scientific analysis, quantitative methods, and technical documentation.

## Areas of Interest

- Engineering Concept Development
- Systems Design and Integration
- Model-Based Engineering
- Engineering Optimization
- Prototype Development
- Technical Feasibility Studies
- Space Systems Concepts
- Computational Design Tools
- Interdisciplinary Engineering Applications

## Project Documentation

Completed projects may include design requirements, mathematical models, source code, CAD files, simulations, engineering drawings, technical reports, and evaluation results.

Only material appropriate for public distribution will be included. Proprietary, commercially sensitive, and restricted research developments will be maintained separately.
