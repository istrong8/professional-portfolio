# Supply Chain Analytics — Data Analysis, Performance and Risk

## Overview

This directory documents independent computational projects involving supply chain analytics, procurement data, operational performance, supplier risk, and engineering decision support.

Projects will use publicly available, synthetic, or independently generated datasets. This is a planned research area; no completed analyses are claimed.

## Research Interests

- Supply chain performance indicators and procurement analytics
- Supplier reliability, on-time delivery, and lead-time variability
- Demand and supply uncertainty
- Supply disruption scenarios and resilience
- Inventory turnover, shortages, and service levels
- Data cleaning, anomaly detection, and dashboards
- Risk-informed operational decision support

## Computational Tools

Python, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Plotly, Jupyter, SQL, and, when appropriate, scikit-learn. Each project will document the tools actually used.

## Planned Computational Exercises

1. **Supply Chain KPI Dashboard** — Explore order fulfillment, lead times, and supplier performance using synthetic data.
2. **Supplier Performance Analysis** — Compare delivery, quality, cost, and variability indicators.
3. **Lead-Time Variability Study** — Quantify lead-time distributions and downstream operational effects.
4. **Supply Disruption Scenarios** — Evaluate the effects of hypothetical supply interruptions.
5. **Data Quality and Anomaly Detection** — Document cleaning and outlier analysis in synthetic procurement records.
6. **Engineering Component Supply Risk** — Analyze illustrative component procurement risk with documented assumptions.
7. **Supply Chain Resilience Indicators** — Compare performance under plausible simulated disruptions.

**All exercises: Planned.**

## Project Documentation Standards

Each completed project will include a problem statement, data provenance and dictionary, metric definitions, mathematical methods, source code, results, plots, sensitivity analysis, reproducibility instructions, limitations, and references.

## Data and Publication Policy

Synthetic data will be labeled clearly and never presented as real supplier records. No employer-owned procurement records, confidential supplier information, proprietary prices, restricted schedules, export-controlled content, or other non-public material will be included.
