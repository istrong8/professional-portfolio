# Operations Research & Optimization

This directory organizes independently authored computational and technical research focused on linear and mixed-integer programming, resource allocation, and scheduling. It is part of a public multidisciplinary engineering portfolio. Projects below are planned, not completed.

## Objectives

- Frame specific research and engineering questions.

- Develop reproducible technical artifacts and original calculations.
- Assess limitations, numerical verification, and uncertainty where applicable.
