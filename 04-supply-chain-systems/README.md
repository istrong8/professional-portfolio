# Supply Chain, Logistics & Systems Engineering

This section presents computational projects involving supply chain management, logistics, inventory optimization, decision analysis, and systems engineering.

These studies focus on applying quantitative methods, mathematical modeling, and computational tools to complex engineering and operational problems.

## Areas of Interest

- Supply Chain Optimization
- Inventory Management and Forecasting
- Logistics and Transportation Modeling
- Engineering Systems Integration
- Reliability-Centered Maintenance
- Operations Research
- Decision Analysis
- Risk-Informed Planning
- Resource Allocation and Scheduling

## Methods and Tools

Projects may use Python, SciPy, pandas, NetworkX, PuLP, Pyomo, and other optimization or systems analysis frameworks.

## Documentation

Projects will include problem definitions, mathematical formulations, data assumptions, computational implementations, results, sensitivity studies, and conclusions.
