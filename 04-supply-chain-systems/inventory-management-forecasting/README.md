# Inventory Management & Forecasting

## Overview
Independent, planned computational projects covering inventory policies, forecasting, safety stock, lead time, and engineered spare-parts availability. No completed results are claimed.

## Planned Studies

1. Economic order quantity and reorder-point model.
2. Demand forecast comparison with clear error metrics.
3. Safety stock versus service-level sensitivity.
4. Monte Carlo stockout analysis with synthetic demand.
5. Critical engineering spare-parts planning.

## Methods
Python, pandas, NumPy, SciPy, time-series methods, and Matplotlib as appropriate. Installation and use will be documented by project.

## Reproducibility
Finished projects will include code, synthetic/public input data, definitions, assumptions, output figures, convergence checks where relevant, and reproduction instructions.

## Publication Policy
No proprietary supply-chain records, employer-owned information, restricted material, or confidential vendor data. Synthetic records will be labeled explicitly.
