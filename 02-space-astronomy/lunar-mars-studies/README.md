# Lunar and Martian Studies — Planetary Environments and Space Nuclear Systems

## Overview

This directory is dedicated to independent computational research and technical studies involving lunar and Martian surface environments, planetary hazards, radiation transport, and engineering applications relevant to future space exploration.

Research interests include the interaction between space nuclear power systems and extraterrestrial environments, with emphasis on numerical modeling, safety assessment, environmental effects, and scientific verification.

This directory serves as a multidisciplinary research area integrating nuclear engineering, planetary science, computational physics, and space systems engineering.

## Research Interests

### Lunar Environment

- Vacuum and near-surface particle transport
- Reduced-gravity effects
- Lunar regolith properties and shielding applications
- Electrostatic dust charging and particle lofting
- Lunar illumination and thermal environments
- Micrometeoroid impact environments
- Lunar seismic hazards
- Radiation exposure and shielding

### Martian Environment

- Low-pressure carbon dioxide atmosphere
- Atmospheric transport and dispersion
- Martian dust and aerosol behavior
- Reduced-gravity particle settling
- Radiation exposure at the Martian surface
- Thermal and environmental variability
- Planetary surface hazards

### Space Nuclear Engineering

- Lunar and Martian fission surface power applications
- Radiation transport and shielding
- Environmental hazard identification
- Conceptual accident scenario modeling
- Reactor–environment interactions
- Model verification, validation, and uncertainty quantification

## Computational Tools

Projects may use:

- OpenMC and Geant4 for radiation transport
- SpiceyPy and Skyfield for planetary geometry
- SpacePy and publicly available space environment data
- Python, NumPy, SciPy, and Matplotlib
- MOOSE and other applicable multiphysics tools
- Geographic information systems and scientific visualization software

Software selection will depend on the physics and engineering requirements of each individual project.

## Planned Research Projects

### Project 01 — Lunar Surface Radiation Environment

Investigate representative radiation environments relevant to lunar surface operations and identify appropriate computational modeling approaches.

### Project 02 — Regolith Shielding Study

Develop a simplified radiation shielding model using representative lunar regolith material compositions and compare attenuation under different geometric assumptions.

### Project 03 — Lunar Environmental Hazard Assessment

Develop an independently documented hazard identification framework for selected lunar environmental conditions and their possible effects on hypothetical engineering systems.

### Project 04 — Martian Atmospheric Transport

Investigate simplified particle transport and deposition behavior under representative Martian atmospheric conditions using appropriate physical models.

### Project 05 — Lunar and Martian Environmental Comparison

Compare selected planetary environmental parameters and evaluate implications for conceptual surface engineering systems.

## Scientific Methodology

Projects will document:

- Source data and references
- Model geometry and material properties
- Numerical methods and simulation settings
- Verification and applicable validation evidence
- Sensitivity and uncertainty analyses
- Scientific figures and computational results
- Model limitations and recommended future work
- LaTeX technical reports and compiled PDFs, where applicable

## Research and Publication Policy

All published materials will be independently developed or explicitly authorized for public release.

No employer-owned, proprietary, export-controlled, restricted, or unpublished collaborative research materials will be included without appropriate authorization.

Conceptual models will be identified as educational or exploratory analyses and will not be represented as validated reactor safety assessments.
