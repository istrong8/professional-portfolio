# Space Science, Astronomy & Astrophysics

This section documents my independent computational research and technical projects in space science, astronomy, astrophysics, and space systems engineering.

My interests include lunar and Martian environments, orbital mechanics, spacecraft dynamics, space radiation, planetary science, and scientific modeling of extraterrestrial environments.

## Research Areas

- **Lunar and Martian Systems:** Planetary environments, surface hazards, radiation exposure, and engineering applications.
- **Orbital Mechanics:** Spacecraft trajectories, orbital dynamics, and mission geometry.
- **Space Radiation:** Cosmic radiation, radiation belts, solar particle events, and environmental modeling.
- **Astronomy and Astrophysics:** Celestial mechanics, astronomical observations, and numerical astrophysics.
- **Planetary Science:** Planetary surfaces, regolith, gravity, and environmental conditions.

## Computational Tools

Projects may use SpiceyPy, SpacePy, Astropy, NASA GMAT, Basilisk, Skyfield, SunPy, REBOUND, and other scientific software.

## Documentation

Each completed project will include, where applicable:

- Scientific objectives and theoretical background
- Numerical methods and assumptions
- Source code and simulation inputs
- Results, figures, and visualizations
- Verification or validation evidence
- LaTeX technical reports and PDFs
- References and limitations

Projects are independent educational and scientific demonstrations unless otherwise indicated.
