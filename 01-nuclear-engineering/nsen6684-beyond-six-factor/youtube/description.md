## Title
Beyond the Six-Factor Formula: Leakage, Spectral Effects & Reactivity Feedback in Compact Space Reactors | NSEN 6684

## Description (paste into YouTube)

Classical neutron life-cycle theory is the foundation for criticality, but compact fast-spectrum space reactors need more. This graduate reactor-physics talk:

- formulates the k-eigenvalue problem;
- derives one-group diffusion and its limits;
- measures diffusion error against exact transport benchmarks with my own verified Monte Carlo code;
- analyzes reflector savings and spectral effects;
- uses published KRUSTY data to examine reflector worth and decompose negative reactivity feedback.

Prepared for NSEN 6684, Idaho State University (Ph.D. program in Nuclear Engineering).

Calculations use idealized LA-13511 benchmark cross sections. They are mathematical illustrations, not predictions for any real reactor. KRUSTY data come from the cited papers. Only public information is used. Views are my own and do not represent any employer.
