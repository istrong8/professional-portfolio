# Roadmap

Planned work, kept separate from finished projects so that nothing in this repository is presented as further along than it is. Items move into the main README only when they have code, results, and documentation.

- **Ku Saga: Planet X — source release.** Upload the v0.3.1 archive (`src/`, `tests/`, `reports/`, `figures/`, results), confirm the reorganized scripts run, and choose a license.
- **OpenMC pin-cell study — write-up.** Reproducible inputs, convergence and verification checks, LaTeX technical report.
- **TLECUIL v0.3.** Independent human review (IR-002), verification tests VT-01 to VT-07, golden-file regression, and a first signed release record. v0.2.1 is released at [istrong8/tlecuil](https://github.com/istrong8/tlecuil).

## Areas being explored

These are topics of study, not projects. They will get their own folders once there is work to show.

- **Reactor physics & nuclear data:** MOOSE, NJOY21, FUDGE, depletion and isotopic analysis, Geant4, OpenGATE
- **Thermal-hydraulics & multiphysics**
- **Safety & risk:** event and fault tree analysis, hazard and consequence analysis, HotSpot, HYSPLIT, RAVEN, SALib, SCRAM, reliability engineering
- **Space & astronomy:** lunar and Martian environments for space nuclear systems, Astropy, Basilisk, GMAT, Skyfield, SpacePy, SpiceyPy, SunPy
- **Scientific computing:** numerical methods, optimization, visualization, reproducible research, scientific machine learning
- **Systems & operations:** systems engineering, operations research, supply chain analytics, maintenance and asset management

Earlier placeholder folders for these topics were removed in favor of this list; they remain in the repository history.
