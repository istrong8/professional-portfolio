Ku Saga: Planet X — orbital dynamics for a fictional system

A numerical study of whether a fictional planetary system — a Sun-like star and a stellar-mass black hole in a binary, with five planets and a moon — could hold together. Newtonian N-body modeling with a velocity-Verlet implementation, REBOUND/IAS15 scenarios, and short-duration cross-checks against SciPy DOP853.

  • Python · REBOUND · SciPy · NumPy
  • Interactive offline viewer
Open the viewer ↗ Methods & limitations

The system is fictional. Short-term agreement between integrators is not evidence of long-term stability or habitability. Not peer reviewed.

v0.2 · Open source

TLECUIL — first-order fission surface power sizer

An open first-order thermal–electrical balance for surface fission power: reactor thermal power, conversion efficiency, waste heat, radiator area, and U-235 burned, at any power level. Sixteen verification tests; the JavaScript and Python engines agree to machine precision.

Benchmarked against four published NASA designs (2010–2025). With one panel-effectiveness factor calibrated on the 2010 reference design, it predicts the two 2025 government designs within 3%; a third falls just outside the ±15% acceptance band, with the reasons documented. Includes a theory document for undergraduates and a V&V plan structured after NQA-1-2024 Subpart 2.7.

Named for tlecuilli — Nahuatl for the three-stone hearth.

  • Python · JavaScript · LaTeX
  • BSD-3-Clause

Writing

“Reframing Nuclear Licensing: The FSAR as a Tool, Not a Barrier”

White paper · NE 5599, Idaho State University · May 2025

Argues that the Final Safety Analysis Report is a strategic tool for advanced reactor licensing rather than a bureaucratic barrier, drawing on the Production Part Approval Process from manufacturing, case studies of NuScale, Oklo, and Kairos Power, NRC hearing and NEPA reforms, and the then-forthcoming 10 CFR Part 53.

Read the white paper PDF · 11 pp. LaTeX source

Written as a course paper in May 2025. The Oklo case study discusses the NRC’s January 2022 denial of the first Aurora application; Oklo’s licensing work has progressed since then.
