§ 02

Why these pages

My day job is DOE safety basis work, and I came to it from commercial reactor engineering, where I led NQA-1 software quality assurance. In 2026 the two regulatory worlds started to meet: DOE-authorized test reactors reached criticality, and the NRC finalized a rule that lets those DOE results feed commercial licenses. These pages are my working map of that crossover, kept in public so others can use and correct it.

Related writing: Reframing Nuclear Licensing: The FSAR as a Tool, Not a Barrier (white paper, May 2025).
