Fuel cycle & conversion

Conversion turns U3O8 from mills into UF6 for enrichment. The United States has one operating plant, so several companies are now racing Raven-Flint for the second. § 05

GAIN & NRIC

GAIN (Gateway for Accelerated Innovation in Nuclear) is DOE-NE's front door to the national labs: technical, regulatory and financial help for developers. NRIC (National Reactor Innovation Center), also housed at INL, runs the test beds and the Launch Pad. All four companies that reached criticality by 4 July worked with NRIC.

GAIN vouchers

Funding goes to the DOE lab, not the company; the company covers at least 20% cost share, in-kind allowed. Four rounds a year. By Feb 2025: 108 vouchers, $38.6M, 65 projects completed.

FY 2026: Round 2 (Apr) Constellation, Nano Nuclear, NuCube. Round 3 (Jun) Aalo Atomics, OrganiCore Nuclear, Raven-Flint, Srijan. Round 4 (Oct) three vouchers.

Tracked companies with vouchers: Antares (ORNL, Sep 2024), Raven-Flint (INL, material control and accountancy for its pilot), Aalo, Nano Nuclear, NuCube.

GAIN status resources

  • Advanced Nuclear Directory, Apr 2026 (GAIN, Third Way, USNIC): every U.S. developer with design and stated pathway.
  • Milestones in Advanced Nuclear and State Nuclear Regulatory Dashboard (GAIN with Envoy Public Labs), listed in the GAIN Resource Kit.
  • GAIN Regulatory News: NRC guidance updates such as ARCAP.
  • Pathways to Commercial Liftoff: Advanced Nuclear (DOE, Sep 2024).
  • DOE facility authorization experience (GAIN workshop slides, 2025).

NRIC test beds at INL

DOME (former EBR-II containment): Radiant Kaleidos first; Deployable Energy named next in line (Oct 2026). LOTUS (former ZPPR cell, MFC): Molten Chloride Reactor Experiment, criticality as early as 2027. Launch Pad INL / U.S.A.: DOE-authorized projects on lab land or private sites.
